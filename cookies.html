Cookies

This page explains what cookies are, which ones we use, and how you can manage them in your browser. We keep it simple: no accounts, no newsletter pop-ups, and no ad trackers.

Last updated: August 26, 2025 · Company location: São Paulo, Brazil

1) Overview

“Cookies” are small text files stored on your device. They help a website function and remember basic preferences. We aim for a minimal and privacy-respectful setup.

2) What are cookies used for?

  • Keep the site working (core navigation, security, load balancing).
  • Remember your language so you don’t have to switch it every time.
  • Basic, aggregated analytics to understand traffic and fix issues. We don’t build profiles.

We do not run advertising cookies and we do not sell your data.

3) Cookie categories we use

Strictly necessary

Required for the site to operate (e.g., page routing, security, CDN cache). These cannot be turned off on the site itself.

Functionality

Remembers your interface choices such as the language you selected (e.g., EN / FR / PT / ES).

Performance/Analytics

Anonymous, aggregated metrics (page loads, device type ranges) so we can improve reliability and speed. No cross-site tracking.

4) Third-party services

We may rely on trusted providers to deliver parts of the Service, for example:

  • Hosting/CDN to serve pages quickly and securely.
  • Payment processing to complete purchases (e.g., our local payment provider). They may set necessary cookies to prevent fraud and process transactions.
  • Basic analytics to measure site performance without creating advertising profiles.

These partners are required to handle data in line with applicable laws and our Privacy Policy.

5) No accounts, no persistent profiles

We don’t run user accounts on the site. We only collect the answers you choose to submit in the quizzes in order to generate your results (see Privacy Policy). We do not attach your answers to a marketing profile.

6) Data retention

Cookies used for functionality and security have limited lifetimes. Analytics data is retained only as long as needed to operate and improve the Service and then deleted or anonymized.

7) Manage cookies via your browser

You can block or delete cookies at any time using your browser settings. Common paths:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Settings/Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

If you block strictly necessary cookies, parts of the site may not work as expected.

8) Changes to this Cookies page

We may update this page to reflect technical or legal changes. If updates are material, we will provide appropriate notice.

Questions about cookies? Email support@matchormoveon.com.

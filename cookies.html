Cookies

This page explains what cookies are, which ones we use, and how you can manage them in your browser. We keep it simple: no user accounts, no advertising trackers, and no unnecessary pop-ups.

Last updated: August 26, 2025 · Company location: São Paulo, Brazil

1) Overview

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They help the website function correctly and remember basic preferences. Our approach is minimal and privacy-respectful.

2) Why we use cookies

  • Essential functionality (navigation, security, performance).
  • Language preference so the site remembers EN / FR / PT / ES.
  • Anonymous analytics to understand usage and improve stability.

We do not use advertising cookies and we do not sell personal data.

3) Cookie categories

Strictly necessary

Required for the website to operate properly (routing, security, infrastructure). These cookies cannot be disabled from the site.

Functionality

Used to remember your preferences, such as selected language.

Performance & analytics

Anonymous and aggregated data only (page views, device type ranges). No cross-site tracking and no individual profiling.

4) Third-party services

We may use trusted third-party providers strictly for:

  • Hosting and content delivery (CDN)
  • Payment processing to complete purchases securely
  • Basic analytics to monitor site reliability

These providers are required to comply with applicable data protection laws and process data only for the intended purpose.

5) No accounts, no profiles

Match or Move On does not require user accounts. The answers you submit in quizzes are used only to generate your compatibility results. We do not create marketing profiles or persistent user identities.

6) Data retention

Cookies have limited lifetimes. Anonymous analytics data is retained only as long as necessary to improve the service, then deleted or anonymized.

7) Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies directly in your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect site functionality.

8) Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy to reflect legal or technical changes. Updates will be posted on this page.

9) Contact

For questions about cookies, contact us at support@matchormoveon.com.

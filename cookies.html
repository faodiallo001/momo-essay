Cookies

We use cookies to make the experience smooth, secure, and useful.

Last updated: August 26, 2025

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to remember information about your visit. We also use similar technologies like local storage.

How we use cookies

  • Strictly necessary — required for security, load balancing, and basic navigation.
  • Performance & analytics — help us understand usage (pages visited, errors) to improve the product.
  • Functionality — remember your choices (language, preferences).
  • Advertising — measure campaigns and avoid irrelevant ads (if we run ads).

Your choices

You can manage non-essential cookies below. Strictly necessary cookies are always on because the site won’t work without them. You can also control cookies in your browser settings.

Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. See your browser’s help pages for instructions. If you block essential cookies, some features may stop working.

Retention

We keep analytics cookies up to 13 months unless you clear them earlier. Preference data may be stored until you remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy. We’ll adjust the “Last updated” date and, if the changes are material, we will notify you where appropriate.

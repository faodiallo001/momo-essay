FAQ

Do I have to pay?

Yes. Creating your Love Passport and taking the Compatibility Test require a one-time payment before starting.

Can I redo it?

Yes. You can update your Passport anytime. The latest version becomes your active profile.

Do you guarantee matches?

No. We don’t promise a perfect match — we give you the clearest picture so you don’t waste time with the wrong person.

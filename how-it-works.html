How it works

Three paths — pick yours.

1 · Love Passport

Unlock your love identity

Unlock your Love Passport — a unique profile of your romantic identity. It’s more than answers: it’s your passport to love, something you’ll want to share with the world (or with the right one 😉).

2 · Compatibility Test

See if you actually match

Answer the questions, send the link to your partner, and you’ll both get your instant score. No waiting, no filters — just the truth about your match. Maybe you’re meant to be… or maybe it’s time to move on to the next 👀.

3 · Fix the Problem

Turn conflict into clarity

In a conflict? Each of you writes your side of the story. We analyze your responses and give you a clear, caring explanation to help you move forward — no judgment, just understanding.

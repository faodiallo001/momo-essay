Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Last updated: August 26, 2025

Scope

This Policy applies to the Match or Move On website and products (Love Passport, Compatibility Test, Fix the Problem).

Data we collect

  • Account & contact — name, email, language preferences.
  • Payment — processed by our payment provider; we receive limited transaction metadata (no card numbers).
  • Product answers — your questionnaire inputs and results.
  • Device & usage — pages viewed, device type, diagnostics, cookies and local storage (see Cookies).

How we use data

  • Provide and improve the Service and features.
  • Generate your Love Passport, Test scores, and summaries.
  • Prevent abuse, secure accounts, and diagnose issues.
  • Communicate with you (service messages, updates, support).

Legal bases

Where required, we rely on one or more of the following: performance of a contract, legitimate interests (safety, improvement), consent (e.g., non-essential cookies), and compliance with legal obligations.

Sharing

We share data with vendors who help operate the Service (hosting, payments, analytics). They must protect it and use it only for our instructions. We may disclose information to comply with the law or to protect rights and safety.

Retention

We keep information only as long as necessary for the purposes described or as required by law. You can delete your content or request deletion (see Your rights).

Your rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Portability where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential processing.

To exercise rights, email support@matchormoveon.com. We may verify your request.

Security

We apply technical and organizational measures to protect data. No method is 100% secure; please keep your links and devices safe.

International transfers

Data may be processed in countries other than yours. Where required, we use safeguards like standard contractual clauses.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under the age at which they can lawfully consent to online services in their country. Do not use the Service if you are under that age.

Changes

We may update this Policy. We’ll change the “Last updated” date and notify you where appropriate.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@matchormoveon.com.
